AKM brings knowledge, skills, commands, plugins, and workflows into one portable tool for AI agents. Connect your bundles, discover what a task needs, and load only the capabilities that help.

More capability. Less infrastructure.

Agent harnesses, RAG stacks, and plugin systems often come with a collection of services to install and maintain. AKM brings capability discovery, knowledge retrieval, memory, and workflow orchestration together around your local filesystem and SQLite, so your library stays portable, readable, and under your control.
